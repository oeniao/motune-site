  # Motune

  **Wrist gestures for music control while you move.**

  Motune lets you control music playback on iPhone from Apple Watch with simple wrist gestures. It is designed for running, walking, gym workouts, and moments when your iPhone is in your pocket, on an armband, or out of easy reach.
</div>

## Product

Motune turns Apple Watch into a gesture remote for music playback. Open your music app, enable gesture control, and use clear wrist gestures to play, pause, or switch tracks without taking out your iPhone.

## Core Features

- Control current music playback on iPhone.
- Recognize wrist gestures on Apple Watch.
- Customize which music action each gesture performs.
- Works without account registration, ads, analytics, or tracking.
