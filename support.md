# Motune Support

If you need help with Motune, please check the notes below before contacting support.

## Requirements

- iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
- Apple Watch running watchOS 11 or later.
- A paired Apple Watch connected to the iPhone.
- Motion and Fitness permission on Apple Watch.
- Health permission if Motune uses a workout runtime to keep gesture recognition active.

## Basic Setup

1. Install Motune on both iPhone and Apple Watch.
2. Open your preferred music app and start playback once.
3. Open Motune and turn on gesture control.
4. Open Motune on Apple Watch to start gesture recognition.
5. Make one clear gesture at a time and return your wrist to a relaxed position before the next gesture.

## Troubleshooting

### Gestures are recognized but music does not change

- Confirm gesture control is enabled on both iPhone and Apple Watch.
- Confirm iPhone and Apple Watch are connected.
- Open your music app and start playback once.
- Keep Motune running on Apple Watch while using gestures.

### Recognition is unstable

- Wear Apple Watch firmly but comfortably.
- Use a clear and fast gesture with enough movement range.
- Return your wrist to a relaxed position after each gesture.
- Avoid performing multiple gestures continuously.

### Apple Watch app is not installed

- Open the Watch app on iPhone.
- Check whether Motune is installed on Apple Watch.
- Reinstall the Watch app if needed.
- Restart iPhone and Apple Watch if installation remains stuck.
