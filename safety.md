# Motune Safety & Privacy

Motune is a convenience tool for controlling current iPhone music playback from Apple Watch with wrist gestures.

## Intended Use

Motune is designed for everyday music-control situations:

- Running, walking, commuting, or training.
- When iPhone is in a pocket, armband, bag, or out of easy reach.
- When gloves or earbud controls make normal playback controls awkward.
- When the user wants clear wrist gestures for previous, next, play, and pause.

## Not For Safety-Critical Use

Motune is not intended for:

- Medical diagnosis or treatment.
- Health analysis.
- Fitness coaching.
- Emergency use.
- Navigation or routing.
- Safety-critical situations.

Users should always pay attention to their surroundings and use Motune only when it is appropriate to interact with music playback.

## Privacy Summary

Motune is designed as a focused local utility:

- No account.
- No ads.
- No third-party analytics SDKs.
- No cross-app tracking.
- Motion sensor data is processed locally for gesture recognition.
- Music playback information is used locally for display and control.
- Optional gesture samples are saved locally only when the user intentionally uses the local sample tool.

## Health Data

Motune does not read heart rate, steps, calories, workout history, activity rings, or other health records.

Workout Mode is started by the user. Health permission is used only when the user starts Workout Mode, so gesture control can remain available during that user-started workout session.
