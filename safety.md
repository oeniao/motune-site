<section class="legal-hero">
  <p class="eyebrow">App Store Review</p>
  <h1>Safety & Privacy</h1>
  <p class="lede">Motune is a convenience tool for controlling current iPhone music playback from Apple Watch with wrist gestures.</p>
</section>

<section class="legal-grid">
  <div class="legal-card accent">
    <h2>Intended Use</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>Running, walking, commuting, or training.</li>
      <li>When iPhone is in a pocket, armband, bag, or out of easy reach.</li>
      <li>When gloves or earbud controls make normal playback controls awkward.</li>
      <li>When the user wants clear wrist gestures for previous, next, play, and pause.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Not For Safety-Critical Use</h2>
    <p>Motune is not intended for medical diagnosis, health analysis, fitness coaching, emergency use, navigation, routing, or safety-critical situations.</p>
    <p>Users should always pay attention to their surroundings and use Motune only when it is appropriate to interact with music playback.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Privacy Summary</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>No account.</li>
      <li>No ads.</li>
      <li>No third-party analytics SDKs.</li>
      <li>No cross-app tracking.</li>
      <li>Motion sensor data is processed locally for gesture recognition.</li>
      <li>Music playback information is used locally for display and control.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Health Data</h2>
    <p>Motune does not read heart rate, steps, calories, workout history, activity rings, or other health records.</p>
    <p>Workout Mode is started by the user. Health permission is used only when the user starts Workout Mode, so gesture control can remain available during that user-started workout session.</p>
  </div>
</section>
