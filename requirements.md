<section class="legal-hero">
  <p class="eyebrow">App Store Review</p>
  <h1>Requirements</h1>
  <p class="lede">Motune requires both iPhone and Apple Watch.</p>
</section>

<section class="legal-grid">
  <div class="legal-card accent">
    <h2>Device Requirements</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>iPhone running iOS 18 or later.</li>
      <li>Apple Watch running watchOS 11 or later.</li>
      <li>Apple Watch paired with iPhone.</li>
      <li>Motune installed on both iPhone and Apple Watch.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Motion Permission</h2>
    <p>Motune uses Apple Watch motion sensors to recognize user-performed wrist gestures for music playback control and gesture practice feedback.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Health Permission</h2>
    <p>Health permission is used only when the user starts Workout Mode. This mode uses a workout session so wrist gesture music controls can remain available during exercise.</p>
    <p>Motune does not read heart rate, steps, calories, workout history, activity rings, or other health records.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Basic Setup</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Install Motune on both iPhone and Apple Watch.</li>
      <li>Open a music app and start playback.</li>
      <li>Open Motune and enable Gesture Music Control.</li>
      <li>Open Motune on Apple Watch.</li>
      <li>Choose Standard Mode or Workout Mode.</li>
      <li>Make one clear gesture, return your wrist to neutral, then pause 1 second.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>
