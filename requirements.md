# Motune Requirements

Motune requires both iPhone and Apple Watch.

## Device Requirements

- iPhone running iOS 18 or later.
- Apple Watch running watchOS 11 or later.
- Apple Watch paired with iPhone.
- Motune installed on both iPhone and Apple Watch.

## Permissions

### Motion

Motune uses Apple Watch motion sensors to recognize user-performed wrist gestures for music playback control and gesture practice feedback.

### Health

Health permission is used only when the user starts Workout Mode. This mode uses a workout session so wrist gesture music controls can remain available during exercise.

Motune does not read heart rate, steps, calories, workout history, activity rings, or other health records.

## Basic Setup

1. Install Motune on both iPhone and Apple Watch.
2. Open a music app and start playback.
3. Open Motune and enable Gesture Music Control.
4. Open Motune on Apple Watch.
5. Choose Standard Mode or Workout Mode.
6. Make one clear gesture, return your wrist to neutral, then pause 1 second.

## Default Gestures

| Gesture | Default Action |
|---|---|
| Rotate wrist outward, return, pause 1 sec | Previous track |
| Rotate wrist inward, return, pause 1 sec | Next track |
| Turn palm upward, return, pause 1 sec | Play |
| Bend palm downward, return, pause 1 sec | Pause |
