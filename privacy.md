# Motune Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-10

Motune is designed to minimize data collection.

## Data Collection

Motune does not collect personal data from this app.

Motune does not use:

- User accounts.
- Advertising identifiers.
- Analytics SDKs.
- Third-party tracking SDKs.
- Cloud storage for gesture recognition.

## Motion Sensor Data

Apple Watch motion sensor data is processed locally on the device for gesture recognition. Motune does not automatically upload motion sensor data.

If a local gesture sample tool is available in the app, samples remain on the device unless the user explicitly exports them.

## Health Permission

Motune may request Health permission to run a workout session so gesture recognition can remain active while the Watch app is in use.

Motune does not read your health history, does not store personal health records, and does not share HealthKit data with third parties.

## Music Playback

Motune controls the current music playback on your iPhone. It does not upload listening history, playlists, library data, or music account information.

Now playing information shown in the app is used locally for display and control.

## Data Sharing

Motune does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

## Data Deletion

Motune does not maintain a remote account database. Local settings and optional local gesture samples remain on your device. You can delete local app data by removing Motune from your iPhone and Apple Watch.
