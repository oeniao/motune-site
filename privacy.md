<section class="legal-hero">
  <p class="eyebrow">App Store Review</p>
  <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
  <p class="lede">Motune is designed as a focused Apple Watch music-control utility with minimal data collection.</p>
  <p class="legal-updated">Last updated: 2026-05-17</p>
</section>

<section class="legal-grid">
  <div class="legal-card accent">
    <h2>Data Collection</h2>
    <p>Motune does not collect personal data from this app.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>No user accounts.</li>
      <li>No advertising identifiers.</li>
      <li>No analytics SDKs.</li>
      <li>No third-party tracking SDKs.</li>
      <li>No cloud storage for gesture recognition.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Motion Sensor Data</h2>
    <p>Apple Watch motion sensor data is processed locally on the device for gesture recognition. Motune does not automatically upload motion sensor data.</p>
    <p>If a local gesture sample tool is available in the app, it is optional and saves labeled Apple Watch motion samples locally only when the user intentionally starts a sample session.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Health Data</h2>
    <p>Motune does not read HealthKit health data.</p>
    <p>If the user chooses Workout Gesture Mode, Motune may request permission to start and write a workout session so wrist music control can remain available during that user-started workout.</p>
    <p>Motune does not read heart rate, steps, calories, workout history, activity rings, or other health records.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Music Playback</h2>
    <p>Motune controls the current music playback on your iPhone. It does not upload listening history, playlists, library data, or music account information.</p>
    <p>Now playing information shown in the app is used locally for display and control.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Data Sharing</h2>
    <p>Motune does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="legal-card">
    <h2>Data Deletion</h2>
    <p>Motune does not maintain a remote account database. Local settings and optional local gesture samples remain on your device. You can delete local app data by removing Motune from your iPhone and Apple Watch.</p>
  </div>
</section>
